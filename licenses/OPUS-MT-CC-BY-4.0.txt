Opus-MT translation models (AI pack: models/opus-mt-de-en, models/opus-mt-en-de)

German-English and English-German Marian NMT models trained by the Language Technology Research
Group at the University of Helsinki on OPUS data:
  Jörg Tiedemann and Santhosh Thottingal. 2020. OPUS-MT — Building open translation services for
  the World. Proceedings of the 22nd Annual Conference of the European Association for Machine
  Translation (EAMT), Lisbon, Portugal.
Source: https://github.com/Helsinki-NLP/Opus-MT
Models: https://huggingface.co/Helsinki-NLP/opus-mt-de-en, https://huggingface.co/Helsinki-NLP/opus-mt-en-de
ONNX export (int8): https://huggingface.co/Xenova/opus-mt-de-en, https://huggingface.co/Xenova/opus-mt-en-de
